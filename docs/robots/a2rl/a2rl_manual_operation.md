# A2RL Drone - Manual Operation

## Overview

Operational placeholder for the A2RL UAV used for drone racing.

## Safety Requirements

- Wear required PPE before operation.
- Verify emergency procedures before startup.
- Keep the racing area clear of people and obstacles.
- Confirm the flight boundary and failsafe behavior before takeoff.

## Pre-Operation Checklist

- [ ] Airframe and propellers inspected
- [ ] Battery health and charge verified
- [ ] Flight controller and radio link checked
- [ ] Video link and racing telemetry verified
- [ ] Racing area secured

## Startup Procedure

1. Power on the ground station and video system.
2. Power on the A2RL Drone.
3. Confirm control link, video feed, and failsafe state.
4. Perform a low-risk control test before entering the course.

## Basic Operation

1. Select the approved racing flight mode.
2. Complete a controlled practice lap.
3. Follow the defined course and maintain the safety boundary.

## Shutdown Procedure

1. Complete the lap or end the active mission.
2. Land and disarm the drone.
3. Power off the drone and ground-station systems.

## Troubleshooting

- Problem: Video or control link missing
- Check: Battery state, antenna connections, and radio frequency
- Action: Do not take off until the link is restored and failsafe is verified

## Maintenance Notes

- Daily checks: Frame, propellers, battery, and radio/video links
- Weekly checks: Firmware and flight-controller calibration status
- Critical consumables: Batteries and propellers
