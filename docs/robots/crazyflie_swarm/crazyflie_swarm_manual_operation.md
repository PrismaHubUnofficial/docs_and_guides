# Crazyflie Swarm - Manual Operation

## Overview

Operational placeholder for the Crazyflie swarm, consisting of four aerial units.

## Platform Type

- Category: Aerial
- Units: 4
- System owner: TBD
- Last validated: TBD

## Safety Requirements

- Wear required PPE before operation.
- Verify emergency procedures before startup.
- Keep the flight area clear of people and obstacles.
- Confirm that all four units are identified before takeoff.

## Pre-Operation Checklist

- [ ] All four airframes inspected
- [ ] Battery health and charge verified for each unit
- [ ] Motion-capture or positioning system available
- [ ] Radio links and identifiers verified
- [ ] Operating area secured

## Startup Procedure

1. Power on the positioning and ground-station systems.
2. Power on the four Crazyflie units.
3. Confirm telemetry and identifiers for every unit.
4. Run a low-risk positioning test before the swarm mission.

## Basic Operation

1. Select the swarm mission and flight formation.
2. Execute the mission at low altitude and reduced speed.
3. Monitor telemetry and separation between units throughout the flight.

## Shutdown Procedure

1. End the active swarm mission.
2. Land and disarm all four units.
3. Power off the units and ground-station systems.

## Troubleshooting

- Problem: One unit is missing from the swarm
- Check: Radio link, battery state, and unit identifier
- Action: Stop the mission and reconnect the unit before restarting

## Maintenance Notes

- Daily checks: Airframes, propellers, batteries, and radio links
- Weekly checks: Firmware and calibration status
- Critical consumables: Batteries and propellers
